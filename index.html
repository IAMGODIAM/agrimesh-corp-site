What we build

Sensing the field can afford

Three proposed capabilities, one system. Everything below is proposed or under development — we describe where we are going, not where we have arrived.

Proposed

Self-healing field mesh

Low-cost sensor nodes that relay for one another. When a node drops, traffic reroutes through its neighbors — the mesh heals itself instead of going dark.

Under development

Hyper-local sensor arrays

Soil moisture, temperature, and microclimate sensing at the row level — built to run for seasons on modest power, in the fields that can least afford downtime.

Proposed

On-device farm intelligence

Readings interpreted where they are taken. The field does not need a data center’s permission to know itself — insight stays on the farm, with the farmer.
